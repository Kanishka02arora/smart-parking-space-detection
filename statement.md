# Problem Statement

## Project Title
**Smart Parking Space Detection and Occupancy Monitoring Using Computer Vision**

## Background & Context
Urban areas and commercial spaces face continuous challenges with parking management, leading to congestion, wasted fuel, and driver frustration. Manual monitoring is inefficient and not scalable.

## Objective
To develop an automated, computer vision-based solution capable of:
1. Identifying parking spaces in visual feeds (images/video).
2. Monitoring and determining occupancy status (Occupied vs. Vacant) in real-time.
3. Providing clear visual feedback for parking space availability.
